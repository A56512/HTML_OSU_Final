gameplay options:
  1. Steam
    • you can play through Steam but it's unofficial
    • you cannot gain custom skins if you use this option
    • Steam doesn't support mods so it's not as user-friendly to play compared to other options
  2. Mobile (iOS/Android)
    • harder to play since it's a smaller screen
    • typically used for when you're outside
    • this mode is currently in beta-testing so it's not widely used
  3. Console
    • this is rarely used
    • requires extra configuration and softwares to install
  4. Online Browser (Web-based)
    • usually used for watching replays
    • most of these sites are fansites
  5. Tablet
    • compatible with many graphic tablets
    • it's very precise and many players use it
    • you have to connect it to your PC and then use it as your input device
  6. PC (Windows, macOS, Linux)
    • many players use this and you can play with a keyboard, mouse, tablet, or touchscreen
    • you must download it from the official osu! website

osu!mania

the osu!mania game mode is a game mode
where you hit specific notes at the right times using
your keyboard. this mode is very helpful to piano
players since it helps them understand rhythm deeper
and lets them use their fingers more

osu!taiko

the osu!taiko game mode is inspired by the traditonal
japanese drumming style, taiko (which literally means "drum" in
japanese. what you need to do is hit the left drum at the
right time the blue "orbs" come and hit the right drum at
the time the red "orbs" come. timing is important and it's a
very good practice for people who play the drums.

osu!catch

osu!catch is a specific mode in the whole osu! game
where you essentially catch these falling circles, known as "orbs" in
the beat of the song at the right timings. catch as
many orbs as you can within the right timings!

osu!

the osu! game mode is the most popular game mode
that always appears on the osu! screen. make sure to tap
the circles at the right time and drag them (if it's said)
without letting go. make sure to tap them at the right time
to get the maximum amount of points.
