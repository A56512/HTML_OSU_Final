There are many other similar games like osu!. In this webpage, you'll learn a lot more of them, and hopefully find one that fits you.

Geometry Dash is a rhythm-based platformer where
users navigate a cube through obstacle-filled levels,
synchronized to music. The image is a level called
Sonic Wave which is one of the hardest levels on the game.

ADoFAI (A Dance of Fire And Ice) is another
rhythm game where 2 circles essentially move along
a tile path. It's a rhythm game many piano players
play as well since it really focuses on that aspect.

Just Shapes & Beats is a game where your
cursor has to move along to the beats of the
music to try to avoid the obstacles you may
face and try to not die in the game.

Beat Saber is a game in VR where you
have to slice red and blue blocks in the
beat of the song. Levels get more complex
and harder as they go on.
